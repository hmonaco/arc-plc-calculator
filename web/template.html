Our estimations based on available info

We simulate many possible outcomes for the 2026 county yield and the national MYA price and compute ARC-CO and PLC payments in each one. The figures above are the average payment across those outcomes and the share of outcomes in which each program pays. They do not change with your inputs, and they differ from the payment at the average yield and price because they include the uncertainty in markets and yields.

Sources: expected MYA prices from the September 2026 USDA WASDE report; county trend yields from USDA FSA and RMA data; benchmark yields, benchmark prices, PLC yields and reference prices from USDA FSA.

Payments are $ per base acre and include the 85% payment-acre factor. ARC-CO uses a 90% revenue guarantee and a 12% maximum payment rate; actual revenue uses the higher of the MYA price and the loan rate. PLC pays the effective reference price minus the higher of the MYA price and loan rate, times the PLC yield. Figures are before sequestration and payment limits. For educational purposes only.
